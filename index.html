The beginning of us

It all started when Maddy and Ju were walking by the bar and asked me to join them on their walk to see their new apartment. And there you were. I don't think either of us could have imagined what would come from that night. You treated me like I was some kind of weirdo and, honestly, you were pretty mean to me hahah. Not exactly the most romantic beginning, but I guess you had to keep me humble somehow. It's funny to think that the girl who gave me such a hard time that night would become someone so important to me. If I could go back and tell that version of myself where we'd be two years later, I don't think I'd believe it. I'm just glad our paths crossed.

Sometimes, the beginnings that mean the most aren't the ones that look impressive from the outside. To my Daphne ♡

Dear Daphne,

I can't believe it's already been two years. 730 days of having you right there on speed dial whenever I needed you. No matter what I was going through, I could always count on you. You've been by my side as I finished university, went through my Iron Ring ceremony, and finally, after weeks of stress, received confirmation that Ericsson had officially offered me the job!

You've supported my music, pushed me to be a better person, and shown me that every day, there's still something new to learn about you and another reason to love you. I couldn't ask for a better person to have by my side, and I think these past two years have shown that. Life is never promised, but I know that whatever happens, I will always love you, and I know you'll always have my back.

More recently, you finally moved into your first apartment, and I couldn't be happier for you. Now that you've also received confirmation that you'll be working full-time on the day shift, you'll be closer to me, it'll be easier for you to get to work, and I'll get to spend more than just weekends with such a lovely girl. I'm so happy to see these things falling into place for you, and I'm excited for everything that comes next.

I can't forget our trip to Portugal. That was an important step in our relationship, and I feel like we absolutely crushed it. I had such a great time with you. The views were absolutely stunning, just like you. Going on our little adventures, just the two of us, gets me excited about the future and wherever life might take us. Japan, Sweden, England... We've only been together for two years, so who knows how many places we'll end up discovering together? Wherever we go, I know I'll be happy as long as I'm there with you.

And then there's Lagos, a trip that gave me some memories I'll never forget. I remember one particular night when I was determined to go to a bar to watch Canada play, just so I could see my friend out there on the pitch. After a little convincing, you decided to tag along, and what followed was probably one of the funniest nights of the entire trip. There was the big guy and his daughter, who couldn't seem to stop ordering Sex on the Beach, and all the other crazy characters huddled together, watching the beautiful game on a beautiful night, with a beautiful girl right beside me. It's funny how some of the best memories come from the simplest plans, and that night is one I'll cherish for as long as I live. Looking back, it's not just the places we've visited or the things we've done that make these moments special, it's getting to experience them with you.

I know I can be hard on you sometimes, and that I push you to do better, save your money, and think about the future. But I hope you know that I do it because I love you and want us to build a stable foundation for the life we want together. I want us to have something solid to build on, so that we can create a future we're both proud of. I'm so proud of the changes you've made and how much you've grown in the relatively short time we've known each other. I hope you know how much I admire that.

And it's not just about what I hope to help you achieve. You've made a million little improvements in my life, too. You've inspired me to start going to the gym again, focus on eating real foods, and take control of my life. You've helped me become a better version of myself, and I'm grateful to have someone beside me who makes me want to keep growing.

I hope to write you many more songs and many more letters like this one. I hope that one day we'll be able to look back at 20, 30, or even 60 letters, alongside 60 photographs taken at the top of this mountain, and remember all the memories, adventures, and little moments that brought us there. I hope we'll be able to look back and see just how much we've grown, both individually and together.

I'll see you again next year, and I can't wait to keep making memories with you until then and beyond. Here's to another year of loving you, supporting each other, and discovering what this life has in store for us.

I love you to the moon and back, my little Chat Rat.

With all my love, today and always,
Philip ♡
